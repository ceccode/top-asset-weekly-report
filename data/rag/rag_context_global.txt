## Report 2026-11-23

As of November 23, 2026, the 46 tracked assets had a combined reported market capitalization of **$39.11 trillion**, up approximately $2.14 trillion, or 5.8%, from November 22. The asset count was unchanged, with no recorded entries or exits.

Apple remained first at $5.08 trillion. NVIDIA rose two places to second at $3.99 trillion, followed by Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon. Bitcoin remained ninth at $1.24 trillion.

### Strategic Reflection
Technology-sector equities occupied three of the top five positions: Apple, NVIDIA and Microsoft. NVIDIA, Broadcom and AMD represented semiconductors in the top 20, while Alphabet and Meta were classified as Communication Services.

NVIDIA’s capitalization rose 31.58%, while Tesla rose two places to sixth with a 12.20% increase. Broadcom fell two places to eighth despite a 2.57% increase. Tesla and Meta were nearly tied at approximately $1.534 trillion each.

### Implications
The top five accounted for **48.71%** of the reported aggregate, up approximately 1.28 percentage points from November 22. The top ten represented **66.64%**, up approximately 1.12 points. Both concentration measures rose after declining in the preceding two reporting periods.

Rank changes reflect relative movements as well as individual capitalization changes: Microsoft and Alphabet each fell one place despite capitalization increases. The aggregate combines equities and cryptocurrency and represents reported market capitalizations rather than distinct underlying economic value.
