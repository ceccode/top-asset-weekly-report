Apple remained first at $5.14 trillion. Microsoft rose one place to second at $3.89 trillion, while NVIDIA fell to third at $3.84 trillion. Alphabet and Amazon rounded out the top five. Bitcoin fell one place to ninth at $1.30 trillion.

### Strategic Reflection
Technology-sector equities occupied three of the top five positions: Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA. NVIDIA, Broadcom and AMD represented semiconductors in the top 20, while Alphabet and Meta were classified as Communication Services.

Microsoft’s capitalization declined 3.59% as it moved ahead of NVIDIA, whose capitalization fell 10.42%. Tesla rose one place to eighth with a 0.80% increase, while Bitcoin’s capitalization declined 13.61%.

### Implications
The top five accounted for **47.88%** of the reported aggregate, down approximately 0.85 percentage points from November 9. The top ten represented **66.07%**, down approximately 0.81 points. Both measures declined, following a divergence in the previous week.

Rank changes reflect relative movements as well as individual capitalization changes: Microsoft rose despite its decline, while Chevron fell one place to 22nd despite a 2.58% increase.
